# Brand and demo artwork

Created 2026-11-14 for Ziad's Padel Hub redesign. No stock assets were purchased or copied from third-party listings. Canva was considered but not used to generate/export these assets.

## Logo

Custom code-native SVG artwork: a white perforated padel racket and yellow ball on a cobalt rounded square. The wordmark pairs navy/white `padel` with blue `hub` and a coral full stop. Typeface is Arial/system sans-serif, with no bundled font files. This is a proposed identity, not a claim of trademark registration or clearance.

- `public/brand/padelhub-mark.svg`: scalable standalone symbol.
- `public/brand/padelhub-logo-light.svg`: transparent lockup for light backgrounds.
- `public/brand/padelhub-logo-dark.svg`: transparent lockup for dark backgrounds.
- `public/brand/padelhub-logo.png`: transparent PNG export for sharing.
- `src/app/icon.svg`: matching favicon.

Keep the symbol upright, preserve proportions and leave at least a quarter-symbol of surrounding space. Use the light or dark lockup to suit its background. The live header uses the same symbol with real text for crisp rendering.

## Equipment artwork

Generated using the built-in `image_gen` tool, not the CLI/API fallback. No reference photographs or external brand assets were supplied. Original generated images are retained in the local Codex generated-images directory; optimized 1200px WebP copies are committed under `public/images/demo`. Do not treat these as photographs, evidence of condition, or depictions of the named demo models. Cards are visibly labeled AI demo imagery, and detail captions explicitly explain the distinction. Replace them with permissioned seller photographs when production listings exist. No third-party stock license is asserted.

Racket and balls fully inside frame with generous breathing room, camera looking down at 45 degrees, high-end independent sports magazine photography. No lettering, logos, people, watermarks or graphic overlays. This is illustrative generated equipment, not a specific branded product.
